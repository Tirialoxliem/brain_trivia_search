Brain Trivia Search Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 25, 2026

Brain Trivia Search ("we," "our," or "us") values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how we protect it, and what rights you have regarding your personal data.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information when you use the app:

  • Account information such as email address, username, and password
  • Gameplay information such as scores, game history, and leaderboard rankings
  • Usage data such as app activity, device information, crash reports, and performance logs
  • Advertising information such as ad identifiers and ad interaction data

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • create and manage your account
  • allow secure sign-in and authentication
  • save gameplay progress, scores, and profile information
  • support leaderboards and ranking features
  • improve app features, performance, and reliability
  • respond to support requests
  • detect abuse and unauthorized access
  • deliver and improve advertising and analytics

3. Data Sharing

We may share information with:

  • Firebase and Google services used for authentication, storage, analytics, and app infrastructure
  • Advertising partners for ad delivery and measurement
  • Service providers under confidentiality agreements
  • Legal authorities when required by law

4. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

This app does not directly use browser cookies. If you access a companion website or support page, cookies or similar technologies may be used to remember preferences, measure traffic, improve performance, and support ad personalization.

5. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect personal information, including secure authentication, access controls, encrypted communications where applicable, Firebase security rules, and App Check protections. No system is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect user data.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, request deletion of, or object to processing of your personal data. You may also request a copy of your data or withdraw consent where required.
